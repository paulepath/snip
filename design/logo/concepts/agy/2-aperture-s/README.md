# Concept 2: Aperture S

An architectural monogram lettermark 'S' sculpted from two interlocking camera crop brackets that embrace a high-visibility focal aperture at their center.

As Snip sits in the system tray and appears across GitHub headers and product documentation, the bold geometric 'S' establishes a distinct brand identity. The negative space between the brackets evokes an instant capture lens, balancing the app family's blue palette with an energetic red focal core.
