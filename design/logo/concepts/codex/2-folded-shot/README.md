# Folded Shot

A snapshot becomes a small folded keepsake: the blue outline and miniature landscape stand for an image you can keep, annotate and share. The green turned corner echoes the app family's success colour and gives the silhouette an identifiable notch. Thick edges and a single large landscape shape keep it legible at tray size.

The lockups use a hand-drawn path wordmark; all SVG backgrounds are transparent.
