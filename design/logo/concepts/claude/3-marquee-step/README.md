# Snip - marquee-step

The mark is the moment Snip exists for: a selection marquee, drawn as chunky dashed segments over a frosted tint, with one of the editor's numbered step badges ("1") dropped onto its corner. Capture plus annotation in one glance, so it says "screenshot tool that you mark up" rather than just "crop". The marquee uses Snip's accent blue (#5AA9F2 dark, #0969DA light) and the badge the editor's red (#F87171 dark, a deeper #CF222E on light so the white "1" holds), and the geometry sits on a 16-unit grid so the dashes stay crisp pixels at 16 px instead of turning to mush.
