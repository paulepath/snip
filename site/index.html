Everything your screenshot needs

Show someone where to look, what to do next, and which part matters.

Pick just the right part

Drag a region, click a window, or press Enter for the whole monitor. The screen stays frozen while you choose.

Point it out

Arrows, boxes, ellipses, a freehand pen and text. Pick a mark to move it or change its colour and size.

A closer look

One key gets you started

Capture, explain and copy, without leaving the flow of what you're doing.

01 / Capture

Freeze the moment.
Choose the bit you need.

Print Screen dims and freezes the screen. Drag out a region, or click a window. Snip saves the shot, puts it on the clipboard and opens the editor.

  • Enter takes the whole monitor; Esc cancels
  • Your Windows captures go to Pictures\Snip
  • Click the tray icon to capture, too
02 / Explain

Less back and forth.
More “right here”.

Add numbered steps, highlight a detail and pixelate private information. Move a mark after you've drawn it, edit the text, or undo and try again.

  • Delete a step and the rest renumber themselves
  • Draw beyond the picture's edge; Copy includes the extra space on white
  • Open an existing picture or paste one from the clipboard
03 / Share

The explanation goes with it.

Ctrl+C copies your finished picture. Ctrl+S saves a PNG. Snip only asks before closing when there are marks you haven't copied or saved.

Beyond the PC screen

Your wall display,
in the same editor.

Use a Pi Dash? When your PC's mouse is on the dash, Print Screen captures the wall display instead. The shot lands on the clipboard and opens in Snip, ready for the same arrows, notes and steps.

Snip finds the Pi Dash desktop app's settings automatically. Dash shots go to Pictures\Pi Dash, and you can switch the integration off from the tray.

No Pi Dash? Print Screen captures Windows as usual.

Download

Ready when you need a shot.

Run the installer. Snip starts in the tray, starts again when you sign in, and keeps itself up to date.

Download for WindowsAll releases

Install once. Keep it handy.

Snip installs for your Windows account. You can remove it through Settings → Apps.

The installer gives Print Screen to Snip by turning off Windows' own screen-capture shortcut. If another app still holds the key, check Settings → Accessibility → Keyboard, then exit and restart Snip.

New versions come from GitHub Releases. Snip downloads updates in the background and restarts to apply them when no editor is open.
